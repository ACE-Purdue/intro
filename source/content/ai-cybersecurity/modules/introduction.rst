Introduction
============

Welcome to the foundation of our immersive AI-cybersecurity education series. In this module, we introduce the core motivations, challenges, and educational gaps that this novel learning paradigm seeks to address.

As AI technologies increasingly underpin both defense and offense in cybersecurity, it becomes crucial to train a multidisciplinary workforce that understands the intricate balance between opportunity and threat. This module sets the stage for a deeper exploration of hands-on, collaborative, and immersive educational strategies.

Key Objectives:
---------------
- Understand the **dual nature of AI** in cybersecurity—its capacity to detect, prevent, and also introduce new threats.
- Identify the **curricular gaps** where AI and cybersecurity are often treated as separate disciplines.
- Explore the **barriers to inclusivity**, especially for learners with diverse academic backgrounds and skill levels.
- Appreciate the need for **proactive and collaborative learning** through real-world, game-based activities.

Topics Previewed:
------------------
- The role of **Minecraft** as a platform to model AI algorithms through visual and interactive tools.
- Usage of real-world cybersecurity datasets such as:
  - `CIC-IDS2017 <https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/ids-2017.html>`_ – for intrusion detection
  - `MalMem-2022 <https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/malmem-2022.html>`_ – for malware classification
  - `CIC-DDoS2019 <https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/ddos-2019.html>`_ – for reinforcement-based DDoS mitigation
- Educational theories underpinning this framework such as:
  - `Connectivism <https://www.itdl.org/Journal/Jan_05/article01.htm>`_
  - `Immersive learning environments <https://bera-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/bjet.12916>`_
  - `Collaborative constructivism <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aei.2020.101104>`_ through team-based tasks

By the end of this module, you’ll be ready to explore advanced AI models hands-on, simulate real-world cybersecurity scenarios, and participate in role-based gamified activities—preparing you for both academic and industry-level AI-cyber defense.
