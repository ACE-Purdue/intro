Team-Based Learning and Hackathon Design
========================================

This module introduces **collaborative, role-based activities** that simulate adversarial dynamics in AI-cybersecurity. These are designed to build intuition, critical thinking, and practical skills.

Role Assignments:
------------------
**Defender/Operator Team:**
- Build AI models for classification and prediction.
- Utilize Explainable AI (XAI) tools to diagnose adversarial risks.

**Attacker Team:**
- Craft adversarial samples using gradient-based, black-box, or GAN approaches.
- Evaluate success through bypass rates and confusion metrics.

Explainable AI (XAI) Toolkit:
- **Sobol Attribution**
- **Integrated Gradients**
- **Saliency Maps**
- **Leave-One-Out Analysis**

Hackathon Blueprint:
----------------------
- Teams are assigned competing roles with access to datasets, model templates, and visual dashboards.
- Challenges are time-bound and reflect real-world attack-defense tasks.

Learning Outcomes:
-------------------
- Experience cybersecurity from both attacker and defender perspectives.
- Apply AI techniques in adversarially rich contexts.
- Develop soft skills like teamwork, adaptability, and leadership.
