This section is designed as an interactive and structured learning resource based on the paper *Cybersecurity Education in the Age of Artificial Intelligence: A Novel Proactive and Collaborative Learning Paradigm*. Learners will explore how immersive, game-based environments and collaborative tasks can foster a deep understanding of AI/ML concepts in cybersecurity.
