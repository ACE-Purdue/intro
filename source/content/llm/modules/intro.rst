
Large Language Models (LLMs)
=============================

Introduces the architecture, training, and use cases of large-scale models such as GPT, BERT, and LLaMA, with attention to their risks and applications in CI.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Prompt and fine-tune transformer models.
- Assess security and bias in LLM outputs.

**Hands-on:** Prompt engineering, safety testing, and building domain-specific LLM applications.
