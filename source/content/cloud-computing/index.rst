Cloud Computing
===============
Cloud-computing is a modern approach to designing, building, and running applications that fully leverage the advantages of cloud computing. It emphasizes scalability, resilience, and agility by utilizing technologies such as containers, microservices, dynamic orchestration, and continuous delivery. Cloud-computing systems are designed to thrive in distributed environments, enabling rapid development, deployment, and iteration.

We will explore cloud-computing principles and architectures through structured labs and real-world projects, building a strong foundation for cloud-first application development.
